# Build Brief: Audience Management & Engagement CRM

## 1. Project Context

We already have an existing client platform consisting of:

1. **Public Website**
2. **Opportunities Management Portal**
3. **This new Audience Management & Engagement CRM**

The public website and Opportunities Management Portal have already been built as separate artifacts/apps in this Replit project.

This CRM is the **third and final application** in the initial platform.

IMPORTANT:

This is NOT a traditional sales CRM.

Do not build:

* sales pipelines
* sales leads
* deal management
* sales forecasting
* traditional customer relationship management workflows
* complex customer accounts
* full marketing automation
* a helpdesk/ticketing system

The client has specifically confirmed that the CRM is intended to provide:

> Audience management, automated communications, segmentation and engagement insights.

The CRM should work alongside the existing website and Opportunities Management Portal so that all three applications share the same underlying data and business logic where appropriate.

# 10. Separate Personal Career Level From Preferences

This is a critical business requirement.

Do NOT treat:

```text
User's career level
```

as the same thing as:

```text
Career levels they are interested in hearing about
```

For example:

```text
User's own career level:
Senior
```

could coexist with:

```text
Interested in:
New Entrant
Mid-Level
Senior
```

This is intentional.

A senior person may run an organisation, network or community supporting new talent and therefore want to receive new entrant opportunities.

The data model must support this distinction.

# 12. Roles vs Development Programmes vs Training

This distinction is important.

## Roles

Roles should support audience/tag matching.

For example:

```text
Role
Craft: Design
Career Level: Senior
```

can match users interested in:

```text
Craft: Design
Career Level: Senior
```

or other relevant combinations.

## Development Programmes

Development programmes should ALSO support audience/tag matching.

For example:

```text
Programme:
Work Experience Programme

Target:
New Entrants
```

A senior audience member who has opted into:

```text
New Entrant opportunities
```

should still receive the notification.

## Training Opportunities

Training opportunities can generally be communicated more broadly.

Do not force training to use the same strict career-level matching rules as roles and development programmes.

The system should therefore distinguish the content types and their notification behavior.

# 14. Example Matching Logic

Consider an audience member:

```text
Name: Jane Doe

Own Career Level:
Senior

Interests:
Design
Technology

Interested Career Levels:
New Entrant
Mid-Level

Content Preferences:
Roles
Development Programmes
```

Now a programme is published:

```text
Work Experience Programme

Type:
Development Programme

Craft:
Design

Target Career Level:
New Entrant
```

The system should identify Jane as a matching recipient because:

```text
Programme:
Design + New Entrant

Jane preferences:
Design + New Entrant
```

Her own career level being Senior does NOT prevent the notification.

# 18. Preference Management

Users should NOT need accounts.

Instead, emails should contain a secure:

> Manage preferences

link.

The link should open a secure preference-management page.

Example:

```text

Name
Email

Your career level:
[Senior]

Location:
[Accra]

Interested in:
☑ Roles
☑ Development programmes
☐ Training

Career levels:
☑ New Entrants
☑ Mid-Level
☐ Senior

[Unsubscribe from all communications]
```

Use secure, non-guessable tokens.

Do not expose user records merely because someone knows another person's email address.

Retain the record for reporting and compliance purposes while respecting the communication preference.
