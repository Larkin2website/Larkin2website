## SECTION 3: Three ways in

### Title
**Three ways in, depending on where you are starting**

### Description
Not everyone arrives from the same place, so we run three separate routes. Find the one that matches where you are now.

---

**Never worked in film or TV**

**First Credit Work Experience** is built for people with no previous professional industry experience. Three days of training before you start, then a paid placement inside a department on the production, then six months of alumni masterclasses after it ends. No CV history required. No contacts required.

- **CTA:** Learn about First Credit → /trainings/first-credit-work-experience/

---

**Already directing, want to move into HETV**

The **Creative Leadership Shadowing Programme: Director Pathway** is for freelance directors with at least one directing or 2nd-unit credit on a distributed feature or an HETV drama. A paid six-week placement, spread across prep, shoot and post.

- **CTA:** Learn about the Director Pathway → /trainings/creative-leadership-shadowing-programme-director-pathway/

---

**Already producing, want to move into HETV**

The **Creative Leadership Shadowing Programme: Producer Pathway** is for freelance producers with at least three narrative producing credits. A paid placement of up to fourteen weeks, across prep, production and post.

- **CTA:** Learn about the Producer Pathway → /trainings/creative-leadership-shadowing-programme-producer-pathway/

## SECTION 4: What we'll tell you straight

### Title
**What we'll tell you straight**

### Description
Uncertainty is one of the reasons people give up on this industry before they start. So here is what we commit to before you apply.

**Every placement on this site is paid.** Work experience, shadowing, crew roles: all of it. We do not run unpaid placements.

**We publish everything openly.** Every opportunity is advertised here and assessed through the published process. You do not need industry contacts. That is not encouragement. It is how the process actually works.

**We will always give you a date.** Every programme tells you when applications close, when you will hear back, and by when you should assume it was not this time. You will not be left refreshing an inbox indefinitely.

**We cannot give individual feedback.** Application volumes are high enough that personal responses to everyone is not realistic, and we would rather say so than promise it and fail. It is not a reflection of your application.

**We do not accept speculative CVs.** Not because we do not want to hear from you, but because running everyone through the same open process is the fairest way we know to do this. Sign up instead and we will tell you the moment something opens.

## SECTION 5: Production & Culture

### Title
**Who gets to work here**

### Description
We believe everyone should have the opportunity to work in film and television, regardless of background, experience level, or personal circumstances. That belief is only worth anything if it changes how we recruit, so it does. Our processes are open, published, and applied the same way to everyone.

We work with training providers, community organisations and regional talent networks to reach people who might not otherwise hear about opportunities like these. We particularly encourage applications from people currently underrepresented across UK film and television.

On set and in the office, we expect respect, collaboration and genuine support. We want crews that reflect the communities we film in, and a working environment where people feel valued and heard, including on their very first day in the industry.

**Roles page.** All five current postings are marked Filled. Three homepage CTAs point there. Recommend either an empty-state message on /roles/ ("Nothing open today, here is how to hear first"), or a dynamic count in the hero CTA, so the promise matches what is behind the click.

**Programme status.** The three programme pages currently read as open until you reach the bottom. Recommend a status flag at the top of each (open, closed, or next round) mirrored on the Opportunities page and in Section 3 above.

**Gaps worth filling when you have the material.** Pay rates or basis. Travel and expenses policy. Named training and community partners. Alumni quotes or case studies from Round 1 of First Credit. Any Season 1 detail you are permitted to publish. Each of these would meaningfully strengthen Sections 2, 4 and 5.
