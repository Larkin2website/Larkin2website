---
name: Programme overview status presentation
description: User direction on status notices and course ordering on Training Courses and Opportunities overview pages.
---

Do not display an unconfirmed-registration notice or badge on the Training
Courses overview. This is a presentation-only change, not confirmation that
registration is open.

**Why:** The user asked to remove the unconfirmed-status notice from the selected
Training Courses section, without requesting changes to registration behavior.

**How to apply:** Keep the course visible through neutral presentation and retain
the underlying registration metadata and Apply-link safety checks.

Courses open for registration must appear first. First Credit Work Experience is
closed and belongs in the closed section, not a separate card above open courses.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested this ordering and placement.

**How to apply:** Group First Credit with the courses using its live registration
status rather than an independently positioned static card; recheck its status if
it changes in future.

Apply the same neutral treatment to unconfirmed listings on the Opportunities
overview: keep the real listing visible without an unconfirmed-status heading,
explanation or status label. Remove the notices about closed application windows
not confirming delivery from public programme listings.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested these notice removals on the Opportunities
page after the Training Courses changes.

**How to apply:** Do not relabel courses with incomplete source dates as open or
closed. The user's later correction also applies to course detail pages: do not
publish an invented “unconfirmed” status or explanatory warning as if it came
from the provider.

If a source provides a course registration form but no complete deadline,
retain access to that form with neutral wording such as “View registration form”.
Missing date details alone must not be presented as provider-confirmed closure.

**Why:** The user challenged the imported status because Larkin did not describe
the course as unconfirmed, then explicitly asked to correct it.

**How to apply:** Keep the source's deadline wording unchanged, let visitors check
availability through the provider form, and retain gating for explicitly closed
courses and known deadline cutoffs. Do not invent missing years or timezones.

Course registration links must lead to the appropriate course form, not the
Larkin website.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested direct form links when correcting the
registration sidebar buttons.

**How to apply:** Verify the course-specific form destination and distinguish
course registration from subscribing to future hub updates.
