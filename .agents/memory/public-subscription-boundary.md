---
name: Public subscription delivery boundary
description: Why subscriptions use saved private links rather than claiming email delivery
---

The public signup work intentionally records interest and consent without sending mail. Do not turn a saved preference into a promise of automated alerts.

**Why:** The approved website feedback scope excluded campaigns and automated alert sending, and no mail provider was connected. A private management link is a limited, honest way to support unsubscribe without email-only removal or disclosing membership.

**How to apply:** Keep the no-delivery limitation visible until an approved provider and verified delivery flow exist. Before adding emails, implement mailbox verification and recovery; an anonymous repeat signup must not reveal membership, gain control of another subscription, or reactivate a stopped subscription.
