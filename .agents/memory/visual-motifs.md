---
name: Larkin2 visual motifs
description: Project-specific guidance for avoiding generic decorative panel treatments.
---

Do not use thick black, grey, or accent-coloured bars across the top of Larkin2 panels.

**Why:** The user considers this treatment generic and AI-looking, especially on light editorial surfaces.

**How to apply:** Create hierarchy with typography, spacing, background tone, status dots, badges, or subtle consistent one-pixel borders instead. Keep thin functional dividers where they clarify structure.
