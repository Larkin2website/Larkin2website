---
name: Public visual-change boundaries
description: Why visual restoration must remain separate from public subscription-management behavior.
---

Delegate visual restoration with narrow file ownership and preserve functional subscription-management components rather than recreating whole pages.

**Why:** A previous broad design rewrite replaced a working token-based unsubscribe page with a static visual stub. The styling appeared complete while the user's ability to manage consent was lost.

**How to apply:** When restoring presentation from history, transplant media, animation and styling selectively; do not restore whole historical pages or ask a design helper to rewrite consent flows.
