description: Reference and intent behind the homepage FAQ-to-footer scroll transition.
---

Use a stationary-footer reveal for the homepage FAQ-to-footer transition, not a scale-down animation on the FAQ content.

**Why:** The requested reference was the “Shows” section at https://www.dickclark.com. Inspection on 2026-09-18 showed the effect comes from a bottom-fixed footer and a spacer measured to its height; the preceding section simply scrolls away.

**How to apply:** Preserve that visual relationship when revising the homepage. Keep footer navigation usable with keyboard, reduced-motion settings, and viewports shorter than the footer; fidelity to the reference must not hide links.
